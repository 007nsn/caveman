Getting started

Thank you for your purchase. Your planner is a single file that runs in your web browser. You don't need an account, an install or an internet connection, and everything you enter stays on your own computer.

  1. Download your file from Etsy

    On Etsy, go to You → Purchases and reviews and click Download Files. You'll get BudgetPlanner.zip.

  2. Unzip it into a permanent folder

    Choose a folder you'll keep, such as Documents, rather than leaving the file in Downloads.

    • Windows Right-click the ZIP → Extract All
    • Mac Double-click the ZIP
    • Chromebook Open the ZIP in Files and drag the contents out

  3. Open the planner

    Double-click BudgetPlanner.html. It opens in your browser: Chrome, Edge, Safari and Firefox all work. Bookmark the page so you can come back to it in one click.

  4. Start tracking

    Add your income and expenses with the form at the top of Transactions. Set monthly limits under Category budgets → Edit, and use Settings to change your currency symbol or categories. Everything saves automatically as you go.

  5. Back up once a month

    Click Backup to save a copy of all your data. Use Restore to load it on another computer or in another browser. Always open the same copy of the file in the same browser: clearing your browser's history and site data also erases what you've entered, unless you have a backup.

Phones and tablets

BudgetPlanner is designed for a computer browser. Phones and tablets open downloaded files in a preview mode that may not run the planner, so for the best experience use it on a computer.
