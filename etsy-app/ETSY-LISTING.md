## Why a web app and not an iOS app

Apple only distributes iOS apps through the App Store — you cannot sell or
deliver an installable iOS app through Etsy. What *does* work (and sells
well) on Etsy is a **self-contained web app**: a single HTML file the buyer
downloads and opens in any browser, including Safari on iPhone/iPad. On iOS
the buyer can use Safari's **Share → Add to Home Screen** to get an
app-like icon. That's the format this product uses.

## Description

**Take control of your money — no subscription, no signup, no ads.**

This is a complete budget planner and expense tracker that runs right in
your browser. Download once, use forever. Your financial data never leaves
your device — nothing is uploaded, tracked, or shared.

**WHAT YOU GET**
One HTML file that works on iPhone, iPad, Mac, Windows, Android — any
device with a web browser. No installation, no account, no internet needed
after download.

**FEATURES**
✔ Track income & expenses month by month
✔ Automatic balance & savings-rate summary
✔ Spending-by-category chart
✔ Set monthly budgets per category with progress bars & overspend warnings
✔ Custom categories and currency symbol (works for $, €, £, ¥ and more)
✔ Light & dark mode
✔ Export any month to CSV (opens in Excel / Google Sheets / Numbers)
✔ One-click backup & restore of all your data
✔ Print-friendly monthly reports
✔ 100% private — data is stored only on your own device

**HOW IT WORKS**
1. Purchase and download the file from Etsy (Purchases → Download Files)
2. Open it in any browser — just double-click, or tap it in your Files app
3. On iPhone/iPad: open in Safari, then Share → "Add to Home Screen" for a
   real app icon
4. Start tracking — everything saves automatically

• This is a DIGITAL product. No physical item will be shipped.
• Data is saved in the browser you use it in. Use the built-in Backup
  button to move data between devices or browsers.
• Due to the nature of digital downloads, all sales are final — but message
